How to interpret this report
These charts describe survey responses. Barrier ratings and reported usage frequency are observational and self-reported. Group differences and correlations do not establish causation or measure actual crash risk. Check the questionnaire, sampling method, category coding, and small subgroup counts before making policy or publication claims.
